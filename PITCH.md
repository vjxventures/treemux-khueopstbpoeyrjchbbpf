# Haggle — AI-Native Marketplace

Craigslist was founded in 1995. Its UX hasn't changed since. **Haggle reimagines Craigslist for 2026** — a marketplace where AI agents handle the entire buy/sell experience, from listing creation to price negotiation.

Sellers describe items in plain English and AI generates optimized listings with smart pricing. Buyers set a budget and negotiation strategy, then watch as two AI agents — each with distinct personalities and tactics — negotiate in real-time with offers, counter-offers, and deal-making streamed live. The result: better prices for buyers, faster sales for sellers, and zero awkward email chains.

Built with Next.js, Claude (Anthropic), and Server-Sent Events for real-time streaming, Haggle demonstrates how AI agents can replace the most friction-heavy part of peer-to-peer commerce — the negotiation itself — making local marketplaces actually pleasant to use.
